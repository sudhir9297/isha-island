# Sea Glass

Implemented from the user-selected concept 07. The actual Three.js scene is the background; no generated screenshot or scene-preview image is used in the menu.

- Transparent lagoon-blue gradient concentrated behind left-aligned ivory navigation.
- Small spaced Port wordmark and wave, thin icons, and a sand-colored dot for the active section.
- Explore is intentionally empty apart from navigation, Resume, and Close.
- Map, notes, controls, and settings appear alongside navigation with a stronger transparent wash for readability.
- On small screens, section content replaces the navigation with a back action. It scrolls independently without hiding Close.
- Menu and map shortcuts, Escape dismissal, focus restoration and trapping, touch steering, and debug-only capture remain available.
- No plum/red theme, split preview card, attached icon tabs, or screenshot artwork from the prior implementation remains in the UI. Historical assets and license are retained on disk.
